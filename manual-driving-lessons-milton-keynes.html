FAQ

Manual driving lesson questions

Quick answers for learners looking for manual driving lessons in Milton Keynes.

How much are manual driving lessons?

Manual driving lessons start from £38 per hour.

Is a manual licence better than automatic?

A manual licence lets you drive both manual and automatic cars, giving you more flexibility.

Is manual harder to learn?

Manual takes a little longer to master clutch and gears, but our step-by-step approach makes it straightforward.

Can I take my driving test in a manual car?

Yes. Passing in a manual gives you a full licence to drive both manual and automatic vehicles.

Do you cover Bletchley?

Yes, manual lessons are available across Milton Keynes, including Bletchley and nearby areas.

Can I use your car for my test?

Yes, subject to assessment and DVSA test-standard driving.
