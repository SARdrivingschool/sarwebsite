Common Support Issues

I can't sign in

Check that you're using the email address, telephone number or login information associated with your SAR Driving School account.

If you still cannot access your account, contact us and we can help investigate the issue.

My lesson isn't showing

Lesson information may need to be added or updated by your driving instructor or SAR Driving School.

Contact your instructor or SAR support if a scheduled lesson is missing or incorrect.

My lesson information is incorrect

If a lesson date, time, duration, instructor or other booking information appears incorrect, contact SAR Driving School so the information can be reviewed.

My progress information is incorrect

Learner progress information is normally recorded by your driving instructor.

If you believe something has been recorded incorrectly, speak to your instructor or contact SAR Driving School.

Notifications aren't working

Check that notifications are enabled for the SAR Driving School app in your iPhone or device settings.

If notifications are enabled but you're still experiencing problems, contact app support.

The app isn't loading properly

Try closing and reopening the app and make sure your device has an active internet connection.

You should also make sure you're using the latest available version of the SAR Driving School app.

Learner Support

Learners can use the SAR Driving School app to access information relating to their driving lessons and progress.

If you have a question about your driving lessons rather than a technical app issue, you can contact your driving instructor directly or contact SAR Driving School.

Instructor Support

SAR Driving School instructors experiencing problems with their calendar, learner records, lesson information or instructor account should contact SAR Driving School support.

When reporting an issue, please include the affected learner or lesson where relevant and a description of what happened.
