Not sure which lesson to book?

New learners usually start with a normal lesson so the instructor can assess your level, confidence and learning style. Block bookings are best once you know the right instructor and lesson type for you.

Do you offer manual and automatic?

Yes, SAR Driving School offers both manual and automatic lessons depending on instructor availability in your area.

Yes. A 10 hour block saves £20 compared with paying £38 per hour, and a 5 hour block saves £5.

Standard lesson prices are shown above. Prices may vary slightly depending on location, travel time and instructor availability.
