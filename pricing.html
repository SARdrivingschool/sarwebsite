Need help choosing?

Not sure which option is best?

Send us your driving experience, location and preferred availability. We’ll help you choose the most suitable lesson type.

Which lesson should I book?

If you are a new learner, start with a normal driving lesson. Your instructor can then advise whether manual, automatic, refresher or block booking is best for you.

Do you offer manual and automatic?

Yes — SAR Driving School offers both manual and automatic lessons, depending on instructor availability in your area.

Are prices fixed?

Standard lesson prices are shown above. Prices may vary slightly depending on location, travel time and instructor availability.

How do I book?

You can book through the booking page or message us on WhatsApp. We’ll confirm lesson type, area, availability and instructor.
