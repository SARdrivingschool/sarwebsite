How to pay

Two ways to pay for your lessons

Block booking rates are available whichever way you pay — the rate is the same. The only difference is who holds your money, and what happens if something goes wrong.

Pay your instructor

£38/hr, or block rates

Bank transfer or cash to your instructor, as most of our pupils do.

  • ✓Full block rates — down to £34/hr on 30 hours
  • ✓No fee at all — you pay the rate and nothing on top
  • ✓Pay as you go, or pay for a block up front
  • —Your balance sits with your instructor, not the company
  • —If you change instructor, unused hours are between you and them

Best if you know your instructor, you are happy paying as you go, or you simply want the lowest possible price.

Recommended for blocks

Pay SAR Driving School

+5% protected balance

Paid to the company in advance. Same block rate, plus protection on the money you have paid.

  • ✓Same block rates — you are not paying more per hour
  • ✓Your hours are held by SAR Driving School Ltd, so they survive if your instructor stops teaching
  • ✓Free instructor swap — your remaining hours move with you
  • ✓Covered by our published refund and cancellation terms
  • ✓Cover arranged if your instructor is ill or on holiday

The 5% is the same however you pay us — card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or bank transfer. It buys protection on your balance, and it is never a charge for using a card. On a 30 hour block that is £51 on top of a block that already saves you £120.

Worth being straight about why the protection exists: if you hand an instructor £1,020 for thirty hours and they later stop teaching, that money is with them, not with us. Paying the company is how you avoid that. Paying your instructor is cheaper. Both are legitimate — pick the one that suits you.

Pricing FAQ

Common questions about lesson prices

Quick answers before you book manual, automatic, refresher or block lessons.

How much are manual driving lessons?

Manual driving lessons are £38 per hour, and less on a block. Paid to your instructor there is no fee. Paid to SAR Driving School it is £39.90 for a single hour, the extra 5% protecting your balance.

How much are automatic driving lessons?

Automatic driving lessons are £38 per hour, and less on a block. Paid to your instructor there is no fee. Paid to SAR Driving School it is £39.90 for a single hour, the extra 5% protecting your balance.

Are block bookings cheaper?

Yes. A 5-hour block saves £5, 10 hours saves £20, 20 hours saves £60 and 30 hours saves £120.

Why does paying SAR cost 5% more?

It is not higher per hour — block rates are identical either way. Paying SAR Driving School adds 5%, which applies whichever method you use and is never a charge for paying by card. It buys protection: your hours are held by the company, they survive if your instructor stops teaching, they move with you if you switch instructor, and they are covered by our published refund terms.

Can I pay a custom amount?

Yes. Use the custom payment option only if SAR Driving School has confirmed a custom amount with you.

How do I book?

You can pay online securely by card, book through the website, or message us on WhatsApp. We’ll contact you to arrange lesson date, time and instructor.
