Common questions

Do you offer automatic and manual?

Yes — all lesson types are available in both automatic and manual. Just let us know your preference when booking.

Standard lessons are 2 hours. Block bookings are split into 2 hour sessions at times that suit you.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover Milton Keynes, Bedford, and Leighton Buzzard. Pick-up and drop-off from your home or another agreed location.

Can I pay in instalments?
