Common questions

Do you offer manual and automatic lessons?

Yes. SAR Driving School offers both manual and automatic driving lessons, depending on instructor availability and your area.

Can I book a block of lessons?

Yes. A 10-hour block booking is available for £360, saving £20 compared with hourly lessons.

Are test day bookings guaranteed?

No. Test day bookings are subject to instructor availability and your driving standard. As DVSA-approved instructors, we only take learners to test when they are ready and safe.

How do I pay?

Payment details will be confirmed after booking. Bank transfer is accepted, and online card payment may be added soon.
