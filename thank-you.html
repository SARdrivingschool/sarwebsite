Thank you — we've got it

Your request has reached the SAR office. Here's exactly what happens next.

  1. We match you

    We check which DVSA-approved SAR instructor covers your postcode, your transmission and the times you gave us.

  2. Your instructor contacts you

    Usually the same day, by call or WhatsApp, to introduce themselves and agree your first lesson. Keep an eye on your phone — the number may be new to you.

  3. Nothing to pay yet

    Agree your first lesson first. Then pay your instructor directly with no fee, or pay SAR with a protected balance — your choice.

Haven't heard from us by the next working day? Message 079 02 02 37 37 on WhatsApp and we'll sort it straight away.
