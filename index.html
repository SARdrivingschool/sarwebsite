More than just driving lessons

SAR is a school, not a single instructor. That changes what you get.

Structured syllabus

Every lesson follows a 53-topic syllabus — from the cockpit drill to multi-lane roundabouts and independent driving — so nothing is left to chance before your test.

Progress tracked

Your instructor records what you've covered and how you're doing after each lesson, in your printed logbook and in your instructor's SAR app, so you always know where you stand.

Cover if life happens

Pay SAR and your hours are protected: if your instructor is ill or moves on, another SAR instructor takes over and your balance carries across.

Local expertise, proven

We don't just say we know Bletchley — we filmed it. Free video guides to the roads and roundabouts around the test centre, made by the instructors who teach them.

Common questions

How much are driving lessons?

Manual and automatic driving lessons start from £38 per hour, with block bookings from £34 an hour.

Do you offer automatic driving lessons?

Yes, automatic driving lessons are available across Milton Keynes and nearby areas.

Do you offer manual driving lessons?

Yes, manual driving lessons are available from £38 per hour.

How does instructor matching work?

Tell us your pickup postcode, whether you want manual or automatic, and when you're free. SAR matches you with a DVSA-approved SAR instructor who covers your area and times, and they contact you directly — usually the same day.

Can I use your car for my driving test?

Yes, subject to assessment and DVSA test-standard driving.

Do you offer refresher lessons?

Yes, refresher lessons are available for parking, roundabouts, motorway driving and confidence building.

Do you cover Bletchley test centre?

Yes. Most SAR pupils take their test at Bletchley, and our free Bletchley Test Centre guides cover the roads and roundabouts around it.
