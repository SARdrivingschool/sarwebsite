Areas learners may benefit from practising

Test routes are not published and examiners vary them, so no road is guaranteed to come up. These are the areas around the centre that SAR instructors make sure their pupils are comfortable with.

Roman Roundabout

The large multi-lane roundabout linking Bletchley to the A5, IKEA and Central Milton Keynes. Two give-way lines on some approaches, faded markings on others, and several ways to be asked through it.

About the test centre

Where it is

Bletchley Driving Test Centre, Block 4, Government Buildings, Wilton Avenue, Bletchley, Milton Keynes MK3 6DH. There is parking at the centre and toilets are available.

Check the centre on GOV.UK for current opening details before your test.

Pass rates

DVSA publishes pass rates for every test centre. Figures move year to year and depend far more on preparation than on the centre itself, so we don't quote a number here — see the official data on GOV.UK.

On the day

Bring your photocard licence. You'll be asked one "tell me" question before you drive and one "show me" question on the move, then around 40 minutes of driving including independent driving by sat-nav or signs and one manoeuvre.

Common questions

Where is Bletchley driving test centre?

Block 4, Government Buildings, Wilton Avenue, Bletchley, Milton Keynes MK3 6DH. There is parking at the centre.

Are these the Bletchley test routes?

No. DVSA does not publish test routes and examiners vary them. These are the roads and areas around the centre that learners may benefit from understanding and practising.

Do SAR instructors cover the Bletchley area?

Yes. Most SAR pupils take their test at Bletchley, and every SAR instructor teaches on these roads regularly. Lessons start from your pickup address in Milton Keynes.

Can I use a SAR car for my test at Bletchley?

If you learn with SAR you normally take the test in your instructor's car. If you already have a test booked and need a car and instructor, see driving test car hire on our prices page.

What happens at the start of the test?

An eyesight check, then one 'tell me' question before you drive and one 'show me' question while driving. Our Show Me / Tell Me guides cover the answers.

Learning to drive around Bletchley?

SAR instructors teach these roads every week. Tell us your postcode, manual or automatic, and when you're free — we'll match you with a DVSA-approved instructor.
