2 Booking & Confirmation

A booking is not confirmed until you have received written confirmation from SAR Driving School via WhatsApp, SMS or email. Submitting a booking request form does not guarantee availability.

  • Lesson availability depends on instructor schedule, area and lesson type
  • We reserve the right to decline any booking request
  • Confirmed lesson times must be agreed between pupil and instructor
  • Block bookings must be used within 12 months of purchase

3 Lesson Cancellation by the Pupil

⚠️ A minimum of 48 hours notice is required to cancel or reschedule a lesson. Cancellations made with less than 48 hours notice will be charged in full.

If you cancel or reschedule a lesson with less than 48 hours notice, the full lesson fee will be deducted from your remaining hours or charged to you directly. This applies regardless of the reason given.

  • Cancellations must be made directly by contacting your instructor or SAR Driving School via WhatsApp or phone
  • Cancellations via third parties are not accepted
  • Repeated short-notice cancellations may result in lessons being suspended or block hours being forfeited
  • If you fail to show for a booked lesson without notice, the full lesson will be charged

4 Lesson Cancellation by SAR Driving School

If we need to cancel a lesson, we will give you as much notice as possible. Where cancellation is made by us, your lesson will be rescheduled or the lesson credit returned to your block.

  • We are not liable for cancellations caused by vehicle breakdown, illness or circumstances outside our control
  • In the case of instructor illness or unavailability, we will attempt to arrange a replacement or reschedule
  • No compensation is payable for cancellations made by SAR Driving School in good faith

5 Payments

Lesson fees are payable as agreed with your instructor before or at the start of each lesson, or in advance for block bookings. Payment methods are agreed at the time of booking.

  • Single lesson fees are due at the time of the lesson
  • Block booking fees are due in full before lessons begin
  • Unpaid lessons may result in future bookings being withheld
  • SAR Driving School reserves the right to update lesson prices with reasonable notice

6 Refund Policy

⚠️ All payments made to SAR Driving School are non-refundable except in the specific circumstances listed below. Dissatisfaction with progress, change of mind, passing your test early or finding another instructor are not valid grounds for a refund.

Refunds will only be considered in the following circumstances:

  • SAR Driving School is permanently unable to provide lessons and no alternative instructor can be arranged
  • A medical condition prevents the pupil from ever driving, supported by a doctor's letter
  • A lesson was paid for but not delivered due to our error, and cannot be rescheduled

The following are explicitly not valid grounds for a refund:

  • Change of mind after booking or paying
  • Deciding to use a different driving school
  • Dissatisfaction with progress speed
  • Passing your driving test and having unused block hours remaining
  • Moving area or changing availability
  • Relationship breakdown with an instructor where lessons can continue with another instructor
  • Lessons cancelled by the pupil with less than 48 hours notice
  • Failure to attend a booked lesson

Any refund requests must be submitted in writing to info@sardrivingschool.co.uk. Refund decisions are at the sole discretion of SAR Driving School and will be responded to within 14 days.

7 Your legal right to cancel online purchases

This section applies where you buy lessons from us online or over the phone, rather than face to face. It sets out rights you have under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013. These rights are in addition to, and are not affected by, our own cancellation and refund terms above.

  • You have the right to cancel within 14 days of the day the contract is made, without giving any reason
  • To cancel, tell us clearly — email info@sardrivingschool.co.uk, message us on WhatsApp, or call 079 02 02 37 37. You may use the cancellation form below but you do not have to.
  • If you cancel in time, we will refund you within 14 days of being told, using the same payment method you used

If you want lessons to start during the 14-day period. We will not begin lessons within the cancellation period unless you expressly ask us to. If you do ask us to start and you then cancel within the 14 days, you will be charged only for the lessons actually delivered up to the point you cancelled, in proportion to the total price agreed. Once the service you bought has been fully delivered, the 14-day right no longer applies.

8 Block Booking Terms

Block bookings are purchased as a bundle of hours at a discounted rate. The following conditions apply:

  • Block hours must be used within 12 months of the purchase date
  • Unused hours after 12 months will be forfeited with no refund
  • Block hours cannot be transferred to another pupil
  • Block hours cannot be exchanged for cash or credit
  • The block booking discount applies only to the bundle purchased and not to additional individual lessons
  • Hours used are deducted at the standard lesson duration regardless of early finish

9 Pupil Responsibilities

As a pupil, you are responsible for the following:

  • Being ready at the agreed pickup point at the agreed time
  • Holding a valid provisional driving licence throughout all lessons
  • Informing SAR Driving School of any medical conditions, eyesight issues or DVLA notifications that may affect your ability to drive
  • Behaving respectfully toward instructors at all times
  • Not attending a lesson under the influence of alcohol, drugs or medication that impairs driving ability
  • Wearing appropriate footwear and being fit to drive at each lesson

SAR Driving School reserves the right to terminate a lesson immediately if a pupil is deemed unfit to drive, behaves inappropriately or puts the instructor or public at risk. A full lesson charge will apply in these circumstances.

  • We are not liable for DVSA test centre changes, cancellations or waiting times

All lessons take place in an insured, dual-control vehicle provided by the instructor. Pupils must not interfere with dual controls, safety equipment or vehicle settings.
