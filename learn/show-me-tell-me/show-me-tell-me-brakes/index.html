Show Me, Tell Me — the brakes question

"Tell me how you'd check that the brakes are working before starting a journey." Three sentences — that is the whole answer.

The question

"Tell me how you'd check that the brakes are working before starting a journey." This is a 'tell me' question, asked at the start of the test before you drive.

Say this

  • Not spongy or slack — the pedal should feel firm, not sink towards the floor.
  • Test them as you set off — a gentle press at low speed, before you build any speed up.
  • It should not pull — if the car pulls to one side under braking, something is wrong.

The answer, in order

All three, in order: not spongy or slack; test them as you set off; it should not pull. Keep it short — the examiner is listening for the points, not a speech.

The series

There are 14 'tell me' and 7 'show me' questions. We cover one a week — one short video each — so you have all 21 before your test.
