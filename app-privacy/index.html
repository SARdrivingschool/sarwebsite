SAR Driving School

App Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how SAR Driving School collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use the SAR Driving School mobile application.

Last updated: 21 August 2026

1. About this Privacy Policy

SAR Driving School is committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of learners, driving instructors and other users of our mobile application.

This policy explains what information may be collected through the SAR Driving School app, why we collect it, how it is used and the choices available to you.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect information including:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Account and login information
  • Your role, such as learner or driving instructor
  • Driving lesson bookings and appointment information
  • Lesson dates, times and durations
  • Instructor and learner scheduling information
  • Lesson progress and training records
  • Driving skills, topics covered and progress notes
  • Driving test information where provided
  • Availability and calendar information entered into the app
  • Messages or support requests sent to SAR Driving School
  • Basic device and technical information required for the app to operate

3. How We Use Your Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage your SAR Driving School account
  • Connect learners with their assigned driving instructor
  • Schedule and manage driving lessons
  • Record completed lessons and lesson duration
  • Track learner progress
  • Help instructors manage their working schedules
  • Provide learners with information about upcoming lessons
  • Maintain accurate training and lesson records
  • Provide customer support
  • Protect the security and integrity of the app
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Improve the app and SAR Driving School services

4. Learner and Instructor Information

The SAR Driving School app may contain separate accounts for learners and driving instructors.

Information relevant to delivering driving lessons may be shared between an assigned learner, their instructor and authorised SAR Driving School administrators.

For example, an instructor may be able to view information relating to their learner's scheduled lessons, lesson history and driving progress.

Learners may be able to view information relating to their own lessons, progress and assigned instructor.

5. Sharing of Personal Information

SAR Driving School does not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary to provide the app and our services, including with:

  • Your assigned driving instructor
  • Authorised SAR Driving School administrators
  • Technology providers used to operate and secure the app
  • Hosting, database or authentication providers
  • Authorities where disclosure is required by law

Any third-party provider used by SAR Driving School is only given the information reasonably necessary to perform its services.

6. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or loss.

Information may be stored using secure cloud infrastructure and databases used to operate the SAR Driving School app.

No digital system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect user information.

7. Data Retention

Personal information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the app and our driving school services, maintain appropriate business records, comply with legal requirements and resolve disputes.

Some information may remain in secure backups for a limited period after account deletion.

8. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their SAR Driving School app account and associated personal information.

To request account deletion, contact us using the details below and include the email address or telephone number associated with your account.

Certain information may need to be retained where required for legal, financial, safeguarding, fraud-prevention or legitimate business purposes.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable UK data protection law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information in certain circumstances
  • Request restriction of certain processing
  • Object to certain uses of your personal information
  • Request a copy of certain information you have provided to us

To exercise any of these rights, contact SAR Driving School using the details below.

10. Children's Privacy

The SAR Driving School app is intended for people using or providing SAR Driving School services.

Driving lessons in the United Kingdom may be provided to users under the age of 18. Where appropriate, information relating to younger users will be handled in accordance with applicable UK data protection requirements.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where doing so would not be necessary or appropriate for providing our services.

11. Notifications

The app may use notifications to provide information such as lesson reminders, booking updates or important account information.

You can control notification permissions through your device settings.

12. Third-Party Services

The app may rely on third-party technology services for functions such as authentication, cloud hosting, databases, notifications or app distribution.

Those providers may process limited technical or account information in accordance with their own privacy policies and their agreements with SAR Driving School.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the SAR Driving School app develops or where legal or operational requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page together with the date it was last updated.
