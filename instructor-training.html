The honest version

What qualifying actually involves

Becoming an approved driving instructor means passing three DVSA exams. It is a real qualification and plenty of people do not make it through. We would rather tell you that now than take your money and find out later.

Part 1 — Theory

100 multiple choice questions and a hazard perception test. You need 85 out of 100, with at least 20 in each of the four sections, plus 57 out of 75 on hazard perception. Unlimited attempts.

Part 2 — Your driving

An advanced driving test to a far higher standard than your learner test. No more than six minor faults and nothing serious. Three attempts only.

Part 3 — Your teaching

A 45-minute lesson with a real pupil, marked on 17 competencies out of 51. You need 31 to pass. Three attempts only — and this is the one that catches most people out.

The two-year clock

Once you pass Part 1, the whole process has to be completed within two years. Use up your three attempts at Part 3 and you must stop teaching immediately and wait until two years after your Part 1 before starting again from scratch. This is why we care more about you being properly ready than about you moving quickly.

What we provide

Training, and somewhere to work afterwards

The 40 hours you need

Before you can apply for a trainee licence you need at least 40 hours of training from a qualified ADI, ten of them in a car, completed within the previous six months. That is what we deliver, and we document it properly on your DVSA paperwork.

A Part 3 specialist

We have an instructor on the team whose focus is Part 3 preparation. Our own instructors have been through it recently and remember exactly where it gets hard.

Trainee licence support

If you want to teach while you train, we will help you apply for a trainee licence and provide the supervision or additional training DVSA requires. You would be teaching under our name, in your own town.

A place when you qualify

Pass Part 3 and there is a franchise place waiting at £50 a week, capped — the same as every other instructor here. No obligation, and no penalty if you decide to go elsewhere.

Straight answers

What we won’t promise you

Some training providers will tell you what you want to hear. Here is where we stand.

We can’t guarantee you a trainee licence

DVSA decides who gets one and they can refuse. We will help you put in the strongest application we can, but nobody can promise you the outcome.

We can’t guarantee you’ll pass

You get three attempts at Part 2 and three at Part 3. We will not put you in for a test we do not think you are ready for, because a wasted attempt costs you more than a delay does.

We won’t quote you a price online

What you need depends on where you are starting from. We would rather work out what you actually need and give you a straight figure than advertise a headline number with conditions underneath it.

We’ll tell you if it isn’t for you

If we do not think this is the right move for you, we will say so. We would rather lose a course fee than take money from someone who will not make it through.
