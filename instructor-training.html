Becoming a driving instructor

Qualifying as an ADI means passing three DVSA exams: Part 1 (theory), Part 2 (driving ability) and Part 3 (instructional ability). Part 3 is where most people come unstuck, and it is the part we know best — we have a dedicated Part 3 specialist on the team and our own instructors have been through it recently.

Part 1 — Theory

Multiple choice and hazard perception. Mostly self-study, and we will point you at the right material rather than sell you something you do not need.

Part 2 — Driving ability

An advanced driving test to a much higher standard than the one you took as a learner. In-car training to get your own driving to that level.

Part 3 — Instructional ability

Teaching a real pupil while an examiner watches. This is the one that fails most people, and it is where our in-house specialist spends their time.

What happens after you qualify

Train with us and there is a franchise place waiting if you want it — the same £50 weekly cap as everyone else. We would rather train instructors who stay than sell courses to people we never see again. There is no obligation either way.

Straight answers

Common questions

What does the training cost?

It depends which parts you need and how much in-car time you want, so we quote properly rather than advertise a headline number. Message us and we will give you a straight figure with nothing hidden.

Do I need my own car?

Qualified instructors joining the franchise teach in their own dual-controlled car. If you are training with us, talk to us about it early — it affects your options.

How long does qualifying take?

It varies enormously with how much time you can give it. DVSA also limits how many attempts you get at Part 2 and Part 3, so going in properly prepared matters more than going in quickly.

Is there a minimum commitment on the franchise?

The terms are set out in your franchise agreement, which you will see in full before you sign anything. Ask us for a copy at any point in the conversation.

Can I teach automatic only?

Yes. Seven of our current instructors teach automatic. Demand is strong and growing in Milton Keynes.

What areas would I cover?

Milton Keynes and the surrounding areas we serve, including Bletchley, Wolverton, Newport Pagnell and Stony Stratford. You tell us where you are happy to work.
