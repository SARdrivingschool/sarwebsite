Good to know

Common questions

How long is it valid?

12 months from the date you buy it. If someone is close to the deadline and hasn't used it, talk to us rather than losing it.

Can I choose the amount?

Yes. The amounts above are the common ones, but a voucher can be for any value you'd like to put towards someone's lessons.

What if they already have an instructor?

Vouchers are for lessons with SAR Driving School. If they're already learning elsewhere, it's worth checking with them first.

Do they need a provisional licence?

They'll need one before their first lesson, but not before you buy. It's a good nudge to get them to apply for it.

Can it go towards a block booking?

Yes. A voucher can cover a block outright or come off the cost of one.

Is it refundable?

Vouchers aren't refundable once issued, but they can be transferred to someone else. Just let us know.
