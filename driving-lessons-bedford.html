Bedford Test Centre

Where your Bedford test actually happens

Bedford practical tests run from Bedford Heights, Manton Lane, Bedford MK41 7NY. It is a relatively new site — the DVSA moved testing here in May 2021 after Cardington closed — so a lot of older route advice you will find online is for a centre that no longer exists.

North of the river

Manton Lane sits on the north side of Bedford, which means candidates spend most of the test on that side of the Great Ouse rather than crossing into the town centre repeatedly.

Mixed speed limits

Routes typically string together 30mph residential streets, 40mph link roads and faster dual carriageway stretches. Reading the limit changes early is where a lot of faults get picked up.

We practise on the real thing

Because your lesson is a 2 hour block, there is time to actually drive the roads around Manton Lane rather than just talk about them.

How lessons in Bedford really work

We would rather be straight with you than take a booking we cannot deliver well. SAR Driving School is a Milton Keynes school. Here is exactly what that means for a Bedford learner.

Your instructor travels out to you

We do not currently have an instructor based in Bedford. Yours drives out from Milton Keynes, which is why Bedford lessons are £43 an hour rather than our standard £38, and why we ask for a 2 hour minimum. Anything shorter and most of the lesson is travel.

Why 2 hours is better anyway

Two hours is long enough to leave the estate roads, cover a full test route, do manoeuvres properly and still debrief. Most learners progress faster on fewer, longer lessons than on a weekly hour.

Cheaper if you can start in Milton Keynes

If you work, study or have family in Milton Keynes, starting your lesson there is £38 an hour with no minimum. Plenty of Bedford learners do exactly this and still take their test at Manton Lane.

We are hiring in Bedford

We are training new instructors who live locally, so Bedford eventually has its own. If that sounds like you, have a look at becoming a driving instructor with SAR.

FAQ

Bedford driving lesson questions

Straight answers for learners looking for driving lessons in Bedford.

How much are driving lessons in Bedford?

£43 an hour with a 2 hour minimum booking, because your instructor travels out from Milton Keynes. If you can start your lesson in Milton Keynes instead it is £38 an hour with no minimum.

Why is Bedford more expensive than Milton Keynes?

It is a travel charge, not a different standard of lesson. Your instructor spends roughly an hour on the road either side of a Bedford lesson. The £5 difference covers that rather than being absorbed into everyone else's price.

Which test centre would I use?

Bedford Heights on Manton Lane, MK41 7NY. Some Bedford learners also choose Bletchley, which we know extremely well — ask us and we will tell you honestly which suits you.

Can you pick me up from a Bedford address?

Yes, within Bedford and the surrounding villages, subject to instructor availability. Send us your postcode and we will confirm before you commit to anything.

Do you have a female instructor available in Bedford?

We have four female instructors, all automatic. Availability for Bedford travel varies week to week, so ask and we will check rather than guess.

Can I use your car for my test at Manton Lane?

Yes, subject to an assessment and you driving at DVSA test standard. We will tell you plainly if we do not think you are ready.
