Booking FAQ

Before you book

Quick answers for learners booking manual, automatic or refresher driving lessons.

How quickly can I start?

We will contact you to arrange the best lesson time, instructor and collection area. Send your availability so we can arrange this faster.

Do you offer manual and automatic?

Yes. SAR Driving School offers manual and automatic lessons, plus refresher lessons for returning drivers.

How much are lessons?

Manual and automatic lessons are £38 per hour, and less on a block. Block rates are the same whether you pay your instructor or pay SAR Driving School — paying us simply adds 5% and protects your balance.

What happens after I pay online?

Once paid, our team will contact you to arrange your lesson date, time and instructor.

Can nervous learners book?

Yes. Lessons are calm, patient and tailored around your confidence and current driving level.

Can I book without paying online?

Yes. You can complete the enquiry form or message us on WhatsApp if you prefer to arrange your lesson first.
