Booking FAQ

Before you book

Quick answers about booking and paying for lessons with SAR Driving School.

How quickly can I start?

Availability depends on your area, lesson type and instructor schedule. Send your available days and times.

Do you offer manual and automatic?

Yes. Manual, automatic and refresher lessons are available subject to local instructor availability.

How much are lessons?

Lessons start from £38 per hour. Our block-booking prices offer better value for regular learners.

Why is the online price higher?

Online card payments include a 5% processing/admin charge. Both prices are shown clearly above.

What happens after online payment?

Our team will contact you to arrange the lesson date, time, instructor and collection area.

Can I enquire before paying?

Yes. Complete the enquiry form or message us on WhatsApp before making a payment.
