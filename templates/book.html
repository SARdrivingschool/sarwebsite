What happens after you send it

  1. We read it the same day

    Your request goes straight to the SAR office — a person, not a queue.

  2. We match you

    Area first, then manual or automatic, then the times you gave us.

  3. Your instructor calls or messages

    To introduce themselves and agree your first lesson.

  4. You pay when you're ready

    Your instructor directly with no fee, or SAR with a protected balance. Nothing to pay up front.
