Two ways to pay

The rate is identical. The difference is who holds your money, and what happens if something goes wrong.

Pay your instructor

Bank transfer or cash, as most of our pupils do. Pay as you go or pay for a block up front.

  • Full block rates — down to £{{ '%g' % (P.blocks[-1].direct / P.blocks[-1].hours) }} an hour on 30 hours
  • No fee at all
  • Your balance sits with your instructor, not the company

Pay SAR Driving School

Paid to the company in advance — card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or bank transfer. Same block rate plus {{ P.sarFeePercent }}%.

  • Your hours are held by SAR Driving School Ltd, so they survive if your instructor stops teaching
  • Free instructor swap — remaining hours move with you
  • Cover arranged if your instructor is ill or on holiday
  • Covered by our published refund and cancellation terms

Worth being straight about why the protection exists: hand an instructor £{{ '{:,}'.format(P.blocks[-1].direct) }} for thirty hours and they later stop teaching, and that money is with them, not with us. The {{ P.sarFeePercent }}% is the same however you pay — it is never a charge for using a card.
