How to use these guides

Watch before you drive it

Each video is filmed from a SAR car on the road itself, with the lane you need highlighted. Watch it once before your lesson and the layout will already feel familiar.

Read the notes

Under every video is a written guide: the layout, what to plan for, and where learners most often pick up faults. It's the same thing your instructor will talk you through.

Drive to the day

Roads and markings change and no road is guaranteed to be on your test. Use the guides to build understanding, then follow the signs, markings and traffic in front of you.
