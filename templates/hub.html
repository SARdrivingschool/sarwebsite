Areas learners may benefit from practising

Test routes are not published and examiners vary them, so no road is guaranteed to come up. These are the areas around the centre that SAR instructors make sure their pupils are comfortable with.

Roman Roundabout

The large multi-lane roundabout linking Bletchley to the A5, IKEA and Central Milton Keynes. Two give-way lines on some approaches, faded markings on others, and several ways to be asked through it.

Redmoor Roundabout

Reading overhead signs and lane arrows to follow the A5 south or Milton Keynes East without changing lanes on the roundabout.

Residential streets

Narrow two-way streets like Regent Street: tight turns in, meeting traffic, bends where positioning matters and forced turns at no-entry signs.

Traffic calming

Speed humps and cushions around Water Eaton and the estates — spotting the sign before the marking and getting your speed right.

About the test centre

Where it is

Bletchley Driving Test Centre, Block 4, Government Buildings, Wilton Avenue, Bletchley, Milton Keynes MK3 6DH. There is parking at the centre and toilets are available.

Check the centre on GOV.UK for current opening details before your test.

Pass rates

DVSA publishes pass rates for every test centre. Figures move year to year and depend far more on preparation than on the centre itself, so we don't quote a number here — see the official data on GOV.UK.

On the day

Bring your photocard licence. You'll be asked one "tell me" question before you drive and one "show me" question on the move, then around 40 minutes of driving including independent driving by sat-nav or signs and one manoeuvre.

Test-day car
